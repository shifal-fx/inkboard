FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you reach out.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Inkboard runs in any modern browser. We also offer desktop apps for Windows and Mac, and mobile apps for iOS and Android.

Can people without an account join my board?

Yes. Share a guest link and anyone can view or edit (depending on the permissions you choose) without signing up.

How many people can work on a board at once?

Up to 300 people can collaborate on a single board in real time on paid plans, and up to 25 on the Free plan.

Does Inkboard work offline?

Our desktop apps let you keep working offline. Changes sync automatically once you reconnect.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All boards are encrypted in transit and at rest, and we're SOC 2 Type II certified. See our security page for details.
